# Diagnostic question bank

Ask at most 2-3 at a time, and only what the brain could not answer. Each function lists its highest-signal questions and what a healthy answer sounds like. Score 1-5: 1 = absent or broken, 3 = works but unmanaged, 5 = deliberate and improving.

## Strategy
- Who is your best-fit customer, and would you turn away a paying customer outside that profile? (Healthy: a named profile and a real example of saying no.)
- What do customers say you do better than the alternatives? (Healthy: a specific, repeated reason, not "service".)
- What are the one or two bets this quarter? (Healthy: few, named, with a kill condition.)

## Offer
- What exactly do you sell, at what prices, and when did you last change a price? (Healthy: crisp list; a price touched in the last year.)
- Which offering makes you the most money per unit of effort? (Healthy: they know, and it is growing.)

## Growth
- Where did your last five customers come from? (Healthy: one or two known channels, repeatable.)
- What are you doing weekly to be found, and what does a lead cost? (Healthy: a consistent motion; cost roughly known.)

## Sales
- How many open deals now, and what is the next action on each? (Healthy: a number without checking; next actions exist.)
- Where do deals die: never reply, price, timing, lost to someone? (Healthy: a pattern, named.)

## Ops
- Walk one order or engagement start to finish. Where does it wait? (Healthy: known waits, owned fixes.)
- What breaks or gets redone most often? (Healthy: shrinking list, not a shrug.)

## Finance
- Revenue, biggest three cost lines, and roughly net profit last month? (Healthy: answers within minutes, from records not memory.)
- Months of runway at current burn? (Healthy: a number; alarm under six.)

## People
- What did the founder spend the week on, and what of it could someone else do? (Healthy: majority of time on highest-value work.)
- What would break if you took two weeks off? (Healthy: little; documented handoffs.)

## Scoring notes

- Evidence beats assertion: a confident answer contradicted by the brain scores on the brain's evidence, flagged for the user.
- Low-confidence scores are honest scores. State them as such and name the data that would firm them up.
