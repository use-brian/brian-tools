# The seven functions (MECE taxonomy)

Every business activity of a small company lands in exactly one function. These boundary rules are the source of truth; when two functions could claim a topic, the rule decides.

| Function | Owns | Boundary rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategy** (consult-strategy) | Direction. Who to serve, differentiation, business model, big bets, strategic partnerships, pivot or exit | Owns positioning (who and why). Messaging and channels belong to growth. Distribution partnerships belong to growth |
| **Offer** (consult-offer) | What you sell. Product or service scope, packaging, pricing | Owns setting the price. Finance owns the consequences (margins, unit economics). Roadmap lives here, not strategy |
| **Growth** (consult-growth) | How buyers find you. Marketing, channels, content, brand, distribution partnerships | Owns awareness through lead. Sales owns lead through close |
| **Sales** (consult-sales) | How buyers buy. Pipeline, deals, qualification, conversion, follow-up | Hands off to growth at top of funnel, to ops after close |
| **Ops** (consult-ops) | How value gets delivered. Fulfillment, customer success, retention, internal process, tooling, admin, legal, compliance | The explicit residue catch-all. Churn after onboarding lives here, not sales |
| **Finance** (consult-finance) | How money flows. Cost breakdown, margins, unit economics, cash flow, runway, fundraising | Owns margin math and capital. Never sets prices (offer). "Can I afford to hire" is here; "should I hire" is people |
| **People** (consult-people) | Who does the work. Hiring, delegation, org shape, founder time | Owns role definition and the delegation ladder. Affordability hands to finance |

## Straddle cases, settled

- Pricing complaint: offer (setting) unless the question is "what did the price change do to margin" (finance).
- "No customers": growth if nobody arrives, sales if they arrive and do not close, offer if they close once and never return because the product missed.
- Churn: ops (delivery-driven retention).
- "Should I raise money": finance. "What would we do with it": strategy.
- "I am overwhelmed": people (founder time) first; ops if the process itself is the sink.
- One giant client dominating revenue: finance diagnoses the concentration; strategy owns the client-mix decision.
